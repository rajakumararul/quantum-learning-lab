What are you seeing?

Every pure single-qubit state maps to a point on the Bloch sphere. The north pole is |0⟩, the south pole is |1⟩, and equatorial states are equal-probability superpositions. The vector encodes the relative phase as well as the measurement probabilities.

Try H → S and observe the rotation from the +X direction to the +Y direction. Try H → T → T for the same effect. You can also change θ and φ directly using the sliders.
